GTM Offerings — how it actually runs

The assessment & readout lifecycle

Four people touch one deal: the content author who builds the instrument, the rep who sends the link, the recipient who answers it, and the rep again who turns the answer into something they can send back. This maps every stage to the real screen, the real Apex, and the real fields that change — read from the deployed code, not summarized from memory.

⚠ No live screenshots here — browser automation is blocked in this environment (a proxy-level limitation, confirmed earlier). Every label below is the literal tab, component, and method name, so you can click through the real app in gtm-dev and match it step for step.

The readout's status lifecycle

This is the part your message asked about directly — how it gets approved, and how it flips back. The short version: approving never happens inside gtmReadoutReview. That's deliberate.

submitForApproval Draft → Pending Approval. Content locks; the banner names who it's sitting with.
recallApproval Pending Approval → Draft. The rep's own undo, before anyone acts on it.
(Approval Process) Pending Approval → Approved. Happens on the Salesforce platform itself — approval email, bell, Approval History, mobile — never in this editor, so a rep can't approve their own readout.
publishReadout Approved → Published. Mints the public token the recipient's link now resolves.
unpublishReadout Published → Approved. Revokes the token first, then lands on Approved — where Return to draft becomes available again.
returnToDraft Approved → Draft only. Never from Published directly — unpublish first, on purpose.
